## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Background Remover – PNG Maker cuts the background out of any photo and gives you a real
transparent PNG in seconds — and it does the whole thing on your phone.

Pick a photo, and the app finds the main subject, erases everything behind it, and shows
you the result on a checkerboard so you can see exactly what became transparent. Save it
to your gallery or send it straight to WhatsApp, Gmail, Telegram, Drive or anywhere else.

WHY THIS ONE

• Truly transparent — the erased area is real alpha, not a white rectangle.
• Works offline — segmentation runs on your device with Google ML Kit. Nothing is
  uploaded, and the app does not even ask for internet permission.
• Keeps your quality — the cut-out is applied to your full-resolution photo, not a
  shrunken preview.
• Your original is never touched — it stays exactly where it was, unchanged.
• No sign-up, no account, no watermark on your image.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Tap "+ Upload Image" and choose a photo from your gallery or files.
2. The app detects the subject and removes the background automatically.
3. Compare before and after with a drag slider, and pinch to zoom in on the edges.
4. Tap "Save PNG" — or share it directly to any app.

BUILT FOR REAL PHOTOS

• People, pets, products, food and objects
• Portrait and landscape
• JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP
• Large photos handled safely, with a clear message instead of a crash
• Adjustable edge softness so hair and fur blend naturally

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

The app uses the Android Photo Picker, so it only ever sees the single image you choose —
it does not request access to your photo library, contacts, messages or location. Working
files are deleted automatically. Nothing about you is collected, stored or shared.

Perfect for product listings, profile pictures, stickers, presentations, invitations,
posters and anywhere else you need a clean cut-out.
```
